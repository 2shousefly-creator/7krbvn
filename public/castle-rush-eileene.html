02 / TRANG BỊ

🛡️ Trang bị & build

Biskuit: any set, Avenger crit 100%; if lacking damage, Bounty crit 100% + Weakness 100%

Chloe: Avenger crit 100%, Weakness 0% (no Weakness, to avoid over-damage)

Lina: Avenger crit 65%, Weakness 88% + crit accessory 10% + Weakness 12% (Lina is the Wave 1 last hit, so her damage must be high)

Nezha and Miho exclusive equipment needs at least 3x Damage Amplification 4% tuning, otherwise their Wave 1 basic attacks cannot 1-hit kill

Potential DEF/HP for Nezha and Miho must be level 30 to survive until the end (Eileene's basic attack on Turn 56; very low survival chance past Turn 63 otherwise).

03 / THỨ TỰ SKILL

🎯 Thứ tự skill từng Wave

Chuỗi rotation do tác giả video ghi trong bình luận ghim (Atas = skill trên / Skill 2, Bawah = skill dưới / Skill 1).

Wave 1: Biskuit buff > Chloe top skill.

Wave 2: Biskuit top skill.

Wave 3: Lina top > Miho bottom > Nezha top > Miho top > Nezha bottom > Chloe top > Miho bottom > Biskuit buff > Nezha top > Nezha bottom > Miho top > Lina top > Chloe top > Miho bottom > Nezha top > Nezha bottom > Miho top. (Atas = top skill / Skill 2, Bawah = bottom skill / Skill 1.)

04 / LƯU Ý

💡 Lưu ý khi đánh

  • Speed tuning: Miho 29 > Nezha 29 > Chloe 20 > Biskuit 19 > Lina 19. Exact speed values are flexible; what matters is Nezha is 2nd and Lina is 5th, and Chloe/Biskuit/Lina speeds are below 25.
  • Extreme rotation: get a high score with the previous rotation first (video -1tt2Mx5JzI, linked in the pinned comment) before attempting this one.
  • Wave 1: Nezha's and Miho's basic attacks must 1-hit kill before Chloe's crit buff is active. A miss fails the rotation immediately, exit and repeat.
  • Damage control in Wave 1: Biskuit/Chloe/Lina damage must be enough for Lina to last-hit the remaining monster, but not over-tuned — if Chloe kills the monster, Chloe's buff cannot activate in Wave 1.
  • If the rotation fails 4 times, just sweep instead.
