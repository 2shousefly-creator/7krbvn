02 / TRANG BỊ

🛡️ Trang bị & build

Biskuit: Gatekeeper or Bounty set, Weakness 73% + Rachel's passive 27% to help target Jave and increase the chance of triggering Counter

If Eileene rarely dies (especially at full potential level 30), Sieg and Biskuit can use Immortal accessories without DTR/block equipment to help her die around turn 60+

Potential DEF/HP must be maxed for Ryan, Rachel and Sieg

Eileene potential DEF/HP level 0-10 only.

03 / THỨ TỰ SKILL

🎯 Thứ tự skill từng Wave

Chuỗi rotation do tác giả video ghi trong bình luận ghim (Atas = skill trên / Skill 2, Bawah = skill dưới / Skill 1).

Turn-20 No Delay variant.

Wave 1: Rachel bottom.

Wave 2: Ryan top.

Wave 3: Ryan bottom > Eileene bottom > Biskuit buff > Rachel top > Rachel bottom > Ryan top > Eileene bottom > Ryan bottom > Rachel top > Rachel bottom > Biskuit buff > Ryan top > Ryan bottom > Rachel top > Rachel bottom > then Option 1 or 2. Turn-20 Delay variant: same until the Turn-44 point, where if Ryan top is still on cooldown, swap to Ryan bottom first, then Ryan top. Option 1 (turns 61-63, if Eileene can die and revive): Eileene bottom > Ryan bottom > Ryan top — the revive animation gives enough time to cycle Ryan's skills. Option 2 (if Eileene cannot die): Eileene bottom > Sieg top > Ryan top. (Atas = top skill / Skill 2, Bawah = bottom skill / Skill 1.)

04 / LƯU Ý

💡 Lưu ý khi đánh

  • Speed tuning: Sieg 33 > Biskuit 31 > Eileene 29 > Ryan 29 > Rachel 29. Rachel must be last, but NOT speed 25 — at speed 25 she would act below boss Jave.
  • Rachel must be TC6. Eileene can be any Transcendence (higher is better).
  • Eileene's potential DEF/HP must be level 0-10: she MUST die on turns 61-63 so her revive animation buys time to cycle Ryan bottom > Ryan top for turn 64. If Eileene is too tanky and Counter RNG is low, she will not die and Ryan bottom cannot be used on turn 64.
  • For Turn 20 to not delay, Counter must trigger at least 2-3 times so Jave uses a skill on Turn 18 and Ryan top is ready on Turn 20. If Counter triggers too little and Jave does not use a skill on Turn 18, immediately change the queue to Ryan bottom > Ryan top > Eileene bottom.
