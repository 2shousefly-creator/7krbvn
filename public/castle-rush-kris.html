02 / TRANG BỊ

🛡️ Trang bị & build

Shane: Avenger set, crit 55-59%, Weakness 40-45%, CDM as high as possible, Boss damage accessory + single refine

Heavenia: Avenger set, crit 83-87%, Weakness 45%, CDM as high as possible, Boss damage accessory + crit refine 10%

Xiao: Avenger/Bounty set, crit 91%, Weakness 88%, CDM as high as possible, crit accessory 10% + Weakness refine 12%

Eileene: Avenger/Bounty set, crit as high as possible, Weakness 88%, crit accessory 10% + Weakness refine 12%

Biskuit: Bounty set, crit as much as possible, Weakness 100%, DoT bleed accessory + crit refine

Potential DEF/HP level 30 (max) is required to survive

Lowering crit/weakness for more CDM is only for competitive PvE players and is risky (if Heavenia's top skill misses, the debuff does not land on the boss).

03 / THỨ TỰ SKILL

🎯 Thứ tự skill từng Wave

Chuỗi rotation do tác giả video ghi trong bình luận ghim (Atas = skill trên / Skill 2, Bawah = skill dưới / Skill 1).

Wave 1: Biskuit top skill.

Wave 2: Heavenia bottom skill.

Wave 3: Eileene bottom > Biskuit buff > Shane top > Heavenia top > Xiao bottom > Shane top > Eileene bottom > Heavenia Awakening > Shane top > Heavenia top > Biskuit buff > Shane top > Xiao top > Xiao bottom > Shane top > Heavenia top > Heavenia bottom > Shane top. (Atas = top skill / Skill 2, Bawah = bottom skill / Skill 1.)

04 / LƯU Ý

💡 Lưu ý khi đánh

  • Speed tuning: Xiao 37 > Biskuit 31 > Eileene 29 > Heavenia 29 > Shane 29. Exact speed values are flexible; what matters is Shane is 5th.
  • Eileene must be Awakened for the extra stats; usable from TC0 as long as her passive skill is upgraded.
  • For a higher score, Xiao's ATK must be 100+ higher than Eileene's so Xiao receives Heavenia's Awakening skill buff — with Xiao's exclusive equipment tuned full Damage Amplification, the score rises 150-200k+ depending on triggered Bleed DoT.
  • Avoid misses as much as possible: too many misses mean the boss uses Skill 1 on Turn 66 instead of Skill 2, and the hit hero will die. On Turn 66 the boss MUST use Skill 2 for the team to survive.
  • Triggering Bleed before Xiao uses her skill is bonus damage.
