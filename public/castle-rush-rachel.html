02 / TRANG BỊ

🛡️ Trang bị & build

Miho: Avenger set, crit/weakness up to 100%

The Biskuit buff must go to Nezha and Jury, NOT to Miho

Jury needs enough ATK tuning to receive the Biskuit buff: TC6 recommended so exclusive equipment can be tuned full 4% Amplification (the author, with only TC2 Jury, needed 3 ATK tunings for Jury to receive the buff)

Potential DEF/HP must be max level (in the video Miho dies on turn 67 even at max potential).

03 / THỨ TỰ SKILL

🎯 Thứ tự skill từng Wave

Chuỗi rotation do tác giả video ghi trong bình luận ghim (Atas = skill trên / Skill 2, Bawah = skill dưới / Skill 1).

Wave 1: Biskuit buff.

Wave 2: Biskuit top skill.

Wave 3: Jury bottom > Nezha top > Miho bottom > Nezha bottom > Miho top > Nezha top > Jury bottom > Nezha bottom > Miho bottom > Nezha top > Biskuit buff > Nezha bottom > Jury bottom > Nezha top > Miho bottom > Nezha bottom > Miho top > Nezha top. (Atas = top skill / Skill 2, Bawah = bottom skill / Skill 1.)

04 / LƯU Ý

💡 Lưu ý khi đánh

  • Speed tuning: Biskuit 31 > Ryan 29 > Jury 29 > Miho 29 > Nezha 29. Exact speed values are flexible; what matters is Ryan is 1st/2nd and Nezha is 5th.
  • Jury must be at least TC4 to use this rotation (TC6 recommended). Below TC4 the score is far behind the previous rotation.
  • Jury Skill 1 must be upgraded: DEF reduction is 29% upgraded vs 20% unupgraded.
  • Wave 1 must finish in 1 turn: after the Biskuit buff, Biskuit's basic attack must trigger Jury's Follow-up attack. If the Follow-up does not trigger or Wave 1 takes more than 1 turn, the rotation fails (Nezha's top skill will not be ready on turn 69) — repeat the battle.
  • Jury Follow-up RNG must trigger often but not too often: too many Follow-ups shift the boss's Skill 2 timing forward, which puts Nezha's skills under a debuff. Boss Skill 2 must come out before Miho uses her bottom skill.
  • With Jury TC6 and full Damage Amplification exclusive equipment tuning, score can reach ~14M depending on equipment and RNG.
  • Author notes they only demonstrate the rotation/build with their TC2 Jury and do not recommend using Jury below TC4.
