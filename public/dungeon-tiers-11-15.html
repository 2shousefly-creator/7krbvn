Power Up Dungeon Tiers 11–15 (đủ 5 nguyên tố) và Gold Dungeon: đội hình từng nguyên tố theo video hướng dẫn của Windgale.

01 / LƯU Ý VỀ VIDEO

⚠️ Video này không phải Tier 6

Mặc dù tiêu đề video dễ gây nhầm, nội dung video này là Power Up Dungeon Tiers 11–15 (đủ 5 nguyên tố) cộng thêm Gold Dungeon. Từ Tier 11 trở lên, phần thưởng có thêm tiền tệ mới Inner Spark, dùng để nâng Potential của Hero (3 nhánh ATK / DEF / HP, mỗi nhánh 10 tier).

Tên “Lania” xuất hiện trong đội Fire và Water của video (nghe được trong phụ đề) — chưa xác nhận là Hero nào, bài viết ghi đúng như trong video. Xem hình trong video để đối chiếu trước khi xếp đội.

02 / ĐỘI HÌNH TỪNG NGUYÊN TỐ

⚔️ Đội hình Tiers 11–15 & Gold Dungeon

Fire (T11-15)

Evan (Awakening, key hero — basic attack 50% Freeze, enchant the basic), Spike (bottom skill 100% Freeze; replaceable with Evan or Rudy for more tankiness), Lina (burst damage), Yui (anti-Burn), plus 'Lania' (name as spoken in the transcript; bottom skill 100% Freeze — see coverage notes)

Water (T11-15)

Fengyan or 'Lania' (anti-Freeze), Katy (Unable to Recover so the mushroom cannot heal; also Physical Vulnerability + Block Rate Reduction), Lina (HP build, survive/heal/buff), Jin (Poison, bottom skill used), Cleo (main DPS; top+bottom skills Poison + Petrify, basic also Poison)

Earth (T11-15)

Rachel (Avenger, semi-damage), Snipper (Lethal set), Yuri (Avenger), Evan (anti-Stun), Lina (heal)

Light (T11-15)

Sun Wukong (plain is fine, taken for the heal), Rachel (main damage), Joker (bottom + top skills; one removes the mushroom's Magic Damage Amplify buff, the other reduces its shield), Lina (heal), Chloe (anti-Shock)

Darkness (T11-15)

Chloe (top skill removes debuff + Crit Rate Boost + ATK Boost), Lina (heal), Leo (anti-Death + Bleed), Soi (bottom skill, 100% Bleed; transcript also renders the name as 'Soy'/'Sonya'), Freyja (basic and skills can Bleed; Assassin set as-is; if no Freyja, use Bidam)

Gold Dungeon

Pascal (main DPS; CDM 312, Magic ATK 4400), Miho (passive: Weakness Chance + Violet Dance magic vulnerability), Biscuit (bottom skill + passive), Lina (top skill, Inspiring Melody), Yoongun (second DPS, replaced Daisy because damage was lacking)

Pet: One pet used for all sections (no magic pet owned); Gold Dungeon section uses Delo (Crit Damage Boost, Potential 1)

04 / LƯU Ý

💡 Lưu ý khi đánh

  • TITLE VS CONTENT: despite the title, this video covers Power Up Dungeon Tiers 11-15 (not Tier 6) plus the Gold Dungeon. Tier 11+ rewards a new currency, Inner Spark, used to upgrade hero Potential (3 tabs: ATK / DEF / HP, 10 tiers each; going from tier 1 to tier 2 needs 60 high element, tier 2 to 3 needs 120 — 180 high element per tab, 540 for all three; 3000 Inner Spark per tab, 9000 total for full Potential).
  • Fire T11-15: mushroom takes huge damage when frozen and shattered. Skill order: Lina top (optional) -> Spike bottom (100% Freeze) -> Evan bottom (85% Freeze) -> 'Lania' bottom (100% Freeze) -> heal -> top-skill rotation.
  • Water T11-15: mushroom heals (can full-heal at T15) and is weak to Poison. Skill order: Katy bottom FIRST (Unable to Recover) -> Cleo bottom/top -> Jin -> heal; only 5 skills, the rest random.
  • Earth T11-15: weak to Fire; can Stun, reduce DEF and HP; damage only from Burn. Skill order: shield first (Earth damage is painful) -> Rachel top -> Snipper bottom -> Yuri bottom -> heal -> Rachel bottom. Only use burn-effect skills (skip Snipper top and Yuri top).
  • Light T11-15: thick shield (70 stacks) + painful damage. Skill order: Joker bottom (reduce shield) -> Joker top -> Wukong top (heal) -> Rachel bottom -> Wukong bottom -> Lina bottom. Once the shield is gone the mushroom becomes soft.
  • Darkness T11-15: mushroom applies Death; damage only from Bleed. Skill order: Leo top (reduce damage) -> Chloe buff removal -> Leo bottom -> Freyja bottom -> Soi heal.
  • Gold Dungeon: not hard if damage is enough; at T1 the enemy one-shots with one skill, so low damage makes it hard. Skill order: Biscuit bottom -> Lina top -> Miho bottom -> Pascal top+bottom -> Yoongun laser. Shown damage (294, 260) was NOT enough to one-shot; the creator notes he does not own 'Pionji' (name as spoken, unclear which hero) and his magic team outdamages his physical team.
