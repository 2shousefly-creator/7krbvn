02 / TRANG BỊ

🛡️ Trang bị & build

Sunran: Avenger, Crit Rate + CDM, clothes all-ATK%, Guts ring; weapon option ideally all-ATK% (his is DEF, kept for PvP).

V: Avenger, boss ring (Weakness Hit Chance);

EE: all-ATK x3 + Damage Amp x1.

Biscuit: Gatekeeper; ring Immortal / Revive / King General element.

Bai Long: Avenger, 100% Crit Rate and Weakness tuned, Guts ring.

Sieg: tanky build, Gatekeeper also fine.

03 / LƯU Ý

💡 Lưu ý khi đánh

  • Ideally bring Stun immunity and Burn immunity, but that makes the run slow — this video uses V instead for a fast burst clear.
  • Boss breath is painful and applies Burn — play fast (16-turn burst).
  • Skill priority (repeat battle): Biscuit bottom -> Bai Long top -> Bai Long bottom -> Sunran skill 4 (Awakening usually not ready yet) -> V top -> Sunran top.
  • Sunran's bottom skill hurts less than her top skill; either is usable.
  • Bai Long dying after using his skills is fine — Sunran finishes the fight.
