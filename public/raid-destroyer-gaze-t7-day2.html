Raid Destroyer Gaze (Tier 7 — Hướng dẫn tài khoản ngày 2 (Jupy)): đội hình, trang bị và lưu ý theo video hướng dẫn của Windgale.

01 / ĐỘI HÌNH

⚔️ Đội hình đánh Destroyer Gaze

HeroVai trò
Evanfront, story hero (free); shield; B6 plain or B5+5
Yuikey hero; heal scales with HP (B+5 recommended); TC2 for Physical Damage Boost; enhance S2 and Revive (3 turns; Revive restores 45% HP on death); passive self-heals when she uses a skill
Rachelfront, story hero (free); B5+5; top skill increases damage / DEF reduction 20% + Physical Damage +17%; bottom skill decreases enemy ATK
Acefront, story hero (free); B5+5; no skills used, only his passive (DEF reduction); top skill adds Physical Damage Taken +17% (usable as the last skill)
Jupyback-row DPS; if you own Shane, use Shane instead (much better); all other heroes are free except Jupy

Trận hình: Protective (Defense 4-1), maxed; Jupy in the back row for the formation damage boost (video states 30%)

02 / TRANG BỊ

🛡️ Trang bị & build

Jupy: Lethal set (main sub ideally Crit Rate; his is CDM, no replacement; still 5-star), clothes with Crit Rate;

Crit Rate 14%, CDM 175%, 2 equip slots unopened.

Evan/Rachel/Ace: tanky equips only — Block Rate / DEF / HP, no ATK needed.

Purple heroes should be TC3 (unlocks the ATK-increase skill and the TC2 skill enhancement;

TC3 adds 14% HP so they survive the dragon's hits).

03 / LƯU Ý

💡 Lưu ý khi đánh

  • Day-2 account guide: Destroyer Gaze is the first raid to AFK-farm (Ox King not yet possible on this account).
  • Stage 7 AFK farms HP items for supports (Yui/Lina) and Lethal items for DPS (Jupy/Snipper/Teo).
  • Skill priority (repeat battle): Yui Revive first (triggers instantly when someone dies) -> Jupy bottom (self-buff/charge) -> Evan shield -> Yui heal -> Rachel top -> Jupy top -> Rachel bottom (decrease ATK). Fixed version adds Ace top as the last skill.
  • Revive placed first can be skipped by the game if nobody is dead — that is normal.
