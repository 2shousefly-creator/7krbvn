02 / TRANG BỊ

🛡️ Trang bị & build

Karon: Gatekeeper, all-ATK% options (his heal scales with all-ATK).

Daisy: Avenger set, Crit Rate 91%, CDM 240, ring adding Crit Rate (reaches 100%).

Espada: EE slot not unlocked yet; needs 60% Crit Rate from EE (40% comes from her own EE buff when allies die); ring single-target ATK DMG boost.

03 / LƯU Ý

💡 Lưu ý khi đánh

  • Ox King summons 4 Kotan — kill them first (Daisy top skill).
  • Losing the speed race is fine.
  • Skill priority (repeat battle): Daisy top (clear summons) -> Biscuit bottom (buff Espada + Daisy) -> Espada bottom -> Daisy bottom (shield) -> Espada top -> heal (Karon or Lina).
  • Daisy must use her bottom skill for the shield before she dies.
